
## How does the job placement support work after completing the program?
```
Part 1: The Career Pipeline
🚀 How Our Post-Training & Internship Job Placement Support Works

At Tinitiate AI, our commitment doesn’t end when your internship finishes. Our program is intentionally structured as a career pipeline:

➡ Paid Training ➡ Fixed-Length Internship ➡ Direct Placement Support

Because you transition directly from rigorous technical training into a practical, hands-on internship, you aren't entering the job market as a typical entry-level applicant. You enter as an experienced professional with a verified track record.
```

```
Part 2: Resume Building & Client Referrals
Here is exactly how we support your placement post-internship:

💼 Production-Ready Resume & Portfolio Building
We don't just list technical skills. We help you translate your specific Tinitiate AI internship projects into high-impact, enterprise-level experience on your resume, showcasing actual data engineering, cloud infrastructure, or full-stack applications you built.

🎯 Targeted Client & Partner Referrals
We actively leverage our corporate network and industry partnerships to share your verified performance metrics and technical profiles directly with hiring managers who trust our training ecosystem.
```

```
Part 3: Interview Prep & Ongoing Support
🛠 Rigorous Mock Interviews & Technical Prep
Navigating corporate hiring requires specific preparation. We conduct technical mock interviews, system design reviews, and live coding practice tailored to the exact roles you are targeting.

⏳ Continuous Placement Assistance
Our placement team works with you to identify relevant openings, provide insider guidance on interview loops, and offer ongoing mentorship until you secure your role.

✨ By the time you complete your internship, you don't just have theoretical knowledge—you have a portfolio of real-world work and a dedicated team pushing your profile to the front of the line!
```
